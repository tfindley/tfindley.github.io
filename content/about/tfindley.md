> A personal venture, run alongside my full-time career — not a contracting shingle. This is where I keep my hands dirty on real infrastructure for real people, and where the creative side of me lives.

**TFindley** started in 2010 as my own practice — my brand, my clients, my standards. Over the years it's spanned two quite different worlds: **IT and networking** for small businesses and individuals, and **photography and multimedia** production. It runs alongside my day job, and always has. It's not staff-augmentation or DevOps-for-hire — it's the place I build and maintain things on my own terms, for people I want to help.

---

## IT & Networking

The technical half is small-scale but genuinely hands-on — solution architecture from design through delivery to ongoing support:

- **Ubiquiti UniFi networks** — full-stack turnkey installs (wired, wireless, and CCTV) that I still run and remotely manage across several live sites today. Real, in-production networks for real users, not just a home sandbox.
- **Structured cabling, rack and patch management** — the physical layer done properly.
- **Remote monitoring and management** of endpoints, servers, and network kit.
- **Multi-OS user support** (Windows, macOS, Linux), user training, and full system lifecycle management.

Maintaining production networks across multiple external sites is a small but real service-provider story: design it, deploy it, and keep it running for people who depend on it — with the same discipline I bring to work.
