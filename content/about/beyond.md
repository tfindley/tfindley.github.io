## Photography & Multimedia

A serious creative pursuit — and one with genuinely **published** work, not just a hobby claim:

- **Wildlife photography** for the **UK Wolf Conservation Trust** — their official 2012 calendar, the front and back covers of *Wolf Print* magazine, and imagery used across their website and promotional material.
- **Event and institutional** photography for Royal Holloway's Information Security Group — including the cover of *"A Brief History of the ISG"*, the ISG newsletter, and Smart Card Centre conference materials.
- **Film and video** — I directed, shot, and recorded sound for *"The Camera Never Lies"*, an interview with **Jeff Widener** — the photographer behind the iconic Tiananmen Square "Tank Man" image — at Royal Holloway.
- Roles across productions: Director, Director of Photography, Sound, 1st Assistant Director, and continuity.

The full creative story and filmography lives on the **[Creative & Film](/photo/creative)** page.
