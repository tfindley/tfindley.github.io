Alongside my technical career, I've built a real body of creative work. It began with **photography** and expanded into **video production, desktop publishing, and audio engineering**.

I started as a Behind-The-Scenes (BTS) photographer in North London's film industry, drawing on the eye I'd developed shooting wildlife. That led to a range of roles on set — Runner, Boom Operator, Camera Operator, Assistant Director, and Second Unit Director. On a film set, the BTS photographer is rarely *just* a photographer: I worked closely with script supervisors and directors on scene management and continuity — work that rewards a careful eye for detail — and stepped up to First and Second Assistant Director on two productions. I also explored wedding and event photography, covering everything from corporate gatherings to sports.

The need to promote myself and build digital assets pulled me into **desktop publishing**, and with it Adobe Photoshop, Illustrator, and InDesign — skills that have been useful far beyond photography.

Running TFindley Photography as an independent practice also meant running a small business — day-to-day operations, finance and accounting, and marketing. It's where a lot of my client-facing and stakeholder instincts were first formed, long before they showed up in my technical roles.
